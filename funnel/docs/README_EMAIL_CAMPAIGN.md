## 💡 Pro Tips for Success

1. **Personalize first name** - 20-30% better open rates
2. **Send at right time** - Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-12pm
3. **Follow up** - 40% of opens happen on 2nd-3rd send
4. **Single CTA** - Only one main button (not multiple)
5. **Mobile-first** - 60%+ opens are on mobile (design is responsive ✅)
6. **Test subject lines** - 50% of success is in the subject line
7. **Track everything** - Can't optimize what you don't measure
