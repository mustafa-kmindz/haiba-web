## How to Pick Which Email to Send

### If your contact is a **Solo Founder / Co-founder**
→ Use **FOUNDER** variant (email-01-founder.html)
- Keywords: "startup," "bootstrapping," "idea," "pre-seed," "pre-revenue"
- Indicators: Founder title, <5 employees, no funding mentioned

### If your contact works in a **Large Company (100+ employees)**
→ Use **INTRAPRENEUR** variant (email-02-intrapreneur.html)
- Keywords: "innovation," "internal," "department," "team lead"
- Indicators: Manager/Director title, works in Innovation/R&D/Emerging Tech
- Job titles: Innovation Manager, Innovation Lead, Emerging Tech, CIO, etc.

### If your contact leads a **Small-Mid Company (20-500 employees)**
→ Use **COMPANY** variant (email-03-company.html)
- Keywords: "business transformation," "digital," "growth," "CEO/COO"
- Indicators: Founder/CEO/Owner titles, owns business decisions
- Company size: 20-500 employees

### If your contact is an **Engineering/Tech Leader**
→ Use **TECH TEAM** variant (email-04-tech-team.html)
- Keywords: "engineering," "product," "dev," "technical"
- Indicators: VP Eng, Engineering Manager, Product Lead, CTO, Tech Lead
- Job titles: VP Engineering, Engineering Manager, Tech Lead, Product Manager
