Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Why is the POC completely free? +
We believe in the power of ideas. We want to remove barriers for innovators. If we can't find a way to work with you on POC, we won't ask you to invest. If the idea is good, we both benefit from scaling it together.
How is this different from hiring a dev agency? +
Typical agencies charge $20-50K upfront for POCs. We build for free. Why? Because we share your vision. If your POC succeeds, we're your partner through scale. We win when you win.
What if the POC shows the idea isn't viable? +
You still have value: real market feedback, technical learnings, and a pivot direction. You saved months and thousands in development. Many successful startups pivot based on POC feedback.
How long does the FREE POC take? +
2-4 weeks for a solid MVP. Depends on complexity. We'll discuss scope in your kickoff call and give you a realistic timeline.
What happens after the POC? +
You have three options: (1) Scale with us to production, (2) Take the code and build yourself, (3) Shelve it. No obligations. If you choose option 1, we discuss production roadmap and investment.
Can I own the code? +
Yes. The POC code is yours. You can use it however you want. If you scale with us, we discuss IP ownership and licensing as part of the production contract.
What types of ideas do you build? +
Any tech idea: AI/ML, web apps, mobile apps, IoT, data platforms, automation, SaaS, etc. Industries: industrial, healthcare, fintech, e-commerce, education, etc. If it's a good idea, we're interested.
Do you sign NDAs? +
Absolutely. We'll sign any reasonable NDA you need before you share your idea.
