Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the POC completely free? −

We believe in the power of ideas. Our mission is to remove barriers for innovators. If your idea is viable and exciting, we both win when it scales to production. We're investing in your success.

How long does a POC take? +

Typically 2-4 weeks, depending on complexity and scope. During your kickoff call, we'll discuss your specific timeline and confirm the POC delivery date.

What if the POC shows the idea isn't viable? +

You still get value: real market feedback, technical learnings, and a clear pivot direction. You saved months and thousands in development. Many successful companies started by validating a different idea first.

Who owns the POC code? +

You do. The POC code is yours to keep and use however you want. If you decide to scale with us, we discuss IP ownership and licensing as part of the production contract.

Can we use your code if the idea fails? +

Yes, absolutely. You own the code. Use it to pivot, improve, or share with investors. There are no strings attached to the FREE POC.

What happens if I need to reschedule? +

No problem! You can reschedule directly through the confirmation email or by visiting your booking link again. We're flexible to work around your schedule.

Do you work with non-technical founders? +

Absolutely! We work with founders at all levels of technical expertise. Our kickoff call is designed to understand your vision and translate it into technical requirements. You don't need to be technical to have a great idea.
